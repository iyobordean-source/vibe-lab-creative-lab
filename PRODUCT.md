# Product

## Problem

The Vibe Lab Creative Lab currently uses a manual application form to hear from people who want to join its volunteer creative community. The intended web experience should make it easy for prospective members to understand the Creative Lab, apply, and know their application was received. It should also make the details easy for the Vibe Lab team to receive and use, without asking them to learn a complicated tool.

## Users

- **Prospective members:** photographers, videographers, designers, content creators, event people, marketers, and other creatives interested in joining.
- **Vibe Lab team:** the people who receive and review applications through a familiar communication channel.

## Goal

Make the path from discovering the Creative Lab to a clearly confirmed application straightforward for a prospective member, while delivering organized application details to the Vibe Lab team with minimal operational effort.

Use the supplied Vibe Lab identity and content. The current tagline is **“Experiences. Creativity. Good Vibes.”** Do not create organizational claims, statistics, testimonials, member benefits, eligibility rules, or achievements that the client has not supplied.

## Core journey

1. A prospective member discovers the Creative Lab and understands what it is.
2. They choose to apply and complete a focused application form.
3. They submit their details and receive a clear confirmation.
4. The application is stored.
5. Its details are prepared in a readable format and sent to the Vibe Lab team through WhatsApp.

The applicant-facing experience should make submission feel clear and complete. The team-facing flow should not depend on an admin dashboard.

## V1 scope

- A distinctive, responsive Creative Lab page using client-supplied identity and content.
- A direct path from that page to an accessible, focused application form.
- Clear field guidance and useful validation.
- A confirmation after successful submission.
- Secure storage of submitted application details.
- A concise, readable WhatsApp handoff to the Vibe Lab contact, using an approved integration and account.

The exact application fields, hosting environment, storage provider, and WhatsApp sending method remain to be confirmed during their respective slices.

## Non-goals

- An admin dashboard, CRM, applicant portal, or team account system.
- Analytics, ranking, scoring, or automated decisions about applicants.
- A broad content-management system or general-purpose form builder.
- Extra workflows that do not help an applicant apply or the team receive an application.
- Invented brand content or unsupported promises about what membership provides.
