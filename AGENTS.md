# Guidance for coding agents

Before making project changes, read PRODUCT.md, DESIGN.md, ARCHITECTURE.md, TODO.md, and README.md.

Preserve the established product and design direction: this is a bold, human, editorial creative-community experience, not a generic SaaS or HR portal. Use client-supplied Vibe Lab identity and content, retain the tagline “Experiences. Creativity. Good Vibes.”, and do not invent claims, statistics, testimonials, or achievements.

Keep the product simple for both applicants and the Vibe Lab team. Follow the documented scope and architecture. Do not add a landing page, form, database, WhatsApp integration, admin dashboard, or other product capability until its TODO slice is active and requested. Keep dependencies minimal and explain any dependency that becomes necessary.
